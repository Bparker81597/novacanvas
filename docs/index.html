How It Works

From Idea to Presentation

Step 1

Upload or Describe Your Work

Start with a sketch, painting, tattoo concept, installation idea, or project description.

Step 2

Get Creative Feedback

Receive guidance on composition, presentation, story, clarity, color, and audience impact.

Step 3

Polish Your Statement

Refine your artist statement, project title, display notes, and social captions.

Step 4

Build Your Portfolio Page

Create a clean shareable page with your work, bio, QR code-ready layout, and contact links.

Step 5

Present With Confidence

Prepare your work for local showcases, community events, ArtPrize, or client presentations.
